1. Project purpose

Pi AutoPrint is a personal engineering project that generates scheduled PDF reports for a privately managed printer. This website describes the project and its handling of Google Sheets information. It is not a public software service, public Google login portal, or hosted motorcycle records viewer. There is no public account-registration function.

2. Authorisation and access

Google Sheets access is available only when the account holder grants permission through Google's OAuth authorisation process. The application requests read-only Sheets access and is intended to use selected values from a configured reporting spreadsheet. Use of Google accounts and APIs is also subject to Google's applicable terms and policies.

Do not use this informational website as a place to submit Google credentials, OAuth tokens, motorcycle documents or other confidential information. No public submission mechanism is provided.

3. Availability and accuracy

Weather, network and financial values shown in generated reports depend on the availability, quality and freshness of their respective data sources. Forecasts may change. Spreadsheet records may be incomplete or out of date. Servicing and renewal reminders are informational aids and should be checked against official records; they are not a substitute for maintenance guidance, insurance documents or statutory notices.

Printing and access to third-party services may fail or be interrupted. No continuous availability or particular result is guaranteed.

4. Local system responsibilities

The operator is responsible for protecting OAuth credentials, keeping the Raspberry Pi and printer secure, reviewing report accuracy, and ensuring printed documents are handled privately. The public informational website does not operate or control another visitor's Google account or physical printer.
